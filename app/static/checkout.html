Incident Shield payment app (demo)

A PayPal-style checkout that scores every payment before any money moves.

Money moves through the PayPal sandbox (order, approval, capture, refund): no real funds. Crediting the payee is a ledger entry in this app. Signals only a telecom, issuer or consortium could see are switched on by you and are marked simulated. Reviewer console · API docs

1. Create a demo account

Try these (after "Load demo history"):

NormalPay demo_shop (name Demo Shop) $20 → allowed, score 0.
Coached scamPay demo_stranger (name Demo Stranger) $45, reference "move to safe account", tick "On a phone call" → held for review, nothing sent to PayPal.
Account takeoverClick "Use a different device", then "Change password", then pay demo_stranger $200 → blocked.
RefundComplete an allowed payment at PayPal, click "Report a problem", then approve it in the reviewer console.
